My name is Ajay Venkatesh. The STERIS posting maps closely onto my work. The stack was \textbf{C\#/.NET}-leaning across the lifecycle: requirements gathering, design reviews, implementation, testing, and deployment alongside architects, product managers, and business stakeholders. The shape of that work, owning Azure-native services for healthcare with regulatory and compliance constraints, is the shape STERIS asks for.

On the full-stack and cloud-native side, at Amazon I built a \textbf{React 18} frontend with optimized API integration, advanced filtering, and client-side caching, paired with backend services in \textbf{Java} (Coral, Smithy, Dagger, CBOR) and unit/integration testing via \textbf{Mockito}. I provisioned the supporting infrastructure with \textbf{AWS CDK} (Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, IAM, CloudWatch) and operated multi-stage, multi-region CI/CD with rollback controls. I'm also fluent in \textbf{Docker} and \textbf{Kubernetes}, and I treat unit testing and code review as part of the build, not an afterthought.

On healthcare and IoT specifically, at NYU's Novel AI Technologies I architected an \textbf{NSF-funded} AI-powered health platform for iOS and Android that ingests real-time wearable telemetry from connected devices, deploying cloud-hosted AI models for health metric analysis and food image processing. Multi-tenant SaaS for regulated workflows is something I've already shipped end-to-end.

What pulls me toward STERIS is the seriousness of the work. Connected medical and life-science devices run on real consequences for patients and clinicians, and the engineering that sits behind them needs to be both careful and modern. That accountability, plus the chance to keep building at the cloud-native and IoT layer, is the environment I want to grow in.

I'd welcome the chance to discuss further. Thanks for considering my application.
