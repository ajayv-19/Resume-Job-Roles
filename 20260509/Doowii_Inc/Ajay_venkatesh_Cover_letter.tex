Doowii's posting hits a few of my strongest threads. At Campus Mesh I built \textbf{CampusIQ}, an LLM-powered AI assistant for students, with iterative \textbf{prompt engineering} and \textbf{RAG retrieval} over structured data and \textbf{embedding-based semantic search}. I've spent enough time in that domain to know that "answer this question about your data" is harder than it looks: schemas drift, users phrase the same question in many ways, and a wrong-but-confident SQL response is worse than no response at all. That's a problem I'd actively want to work on.

Stack-wise, I'm fluent in \textbf{Python} for backend and ML pipelines, \textbf{SQL} for the analytical layer (PostgreSQL, MySQL, BigQuery, DynamoDB), and \textbf{JavaScript / TypeScript / React} for the product surface. The early-career framing fits where I am --- finishing my M.S. in May 2026 --- and the role's mix of product features, data modeling, and integrations is exactly the kind of breadth I want to keep building.

I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I could contribute to Doowii's engineering team. Thanks for considering my application.
